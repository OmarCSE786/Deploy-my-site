Discover the Cultural Heart of India - Kolkata

About Kolkata

Kolkata, formerly known as Calcutta, is the capital of West Bengal. Known as the "City of Joy," it offers a rich blend of culture, heritage, literature, and food. Explore colonial architecture, vibrant festivals, and soul-touching traditions.

Popular Attractions

Howrah Bridge

One of the world's longest cantilever bridges, this engineering marvel spans the Hooghly River and stands as an icon of Kolkata.

Victoria Memorial

A stunning marble monument dedicated to Queen Victoria, showcasing colonial art and history within its museum walls.

Dakshineswar Temple

A revered temple on the Hooghly River’s banks, dedicated to Goddess Kali, known for its serene spiritual ambiance.

Tourist Tips

  • Try local favorites like Puchka, Kathi Rolls, and Mishti Doi.
  • Use app-based rides for safety and convenience.
  • Respect customs at religious and heritage sites.
  • Best time to visit: October to March.
  • Carry bottled water and dress comfortably for the weather.
