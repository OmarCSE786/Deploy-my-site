Discover the Cultural Heart of India - Kolkata

About Kolkata

Kolkata, formerly known as Calcutta, is the capital of the Indian state of West Bengal. Known as the "City of Joy," it is a melting pot of rich culture, heritage, and history. From colonial architecture and bustling markets to literary festivals and mouth-watering street food, Kolkata offers a unique blend of old-world charm and modern vibrancy.

Popular Attractions

Howrah Bridge

One of the world's longest cantilever bridges spanning the Hooghly River, an iconic engineering marvel and symbol of Kolkata.

Victoria Memorial

A grand marble monument dedicated to Queen Victoria, surrounded by gardens and housing a museum with fascinating exhibits.

Dakshineswar Kali Temple

A famous Hindu temple dedicated to goddess Kali located on the eastern bank of the Hooghly River.

Tourist Tips

  • Try the local street food like Puchka and Kathi Rolls from clean places.
  • Use app-based cabs or authorized taxis for comfortable travel within the city.
  • Respect local customs, especially around religious sites like temples and churches.
  • The best time to visit Kolkata is between October and March to enjoy pleasant weather.
  • Carry a water bottle and stay hydrated during outdoor sightseeing.
